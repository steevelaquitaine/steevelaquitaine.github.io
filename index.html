Steeve Laquitaine, PhD

Research scientist at Sorbonne Université, Affiliate at Stanford University, ex-EPFL

I study efficient inference in biological & artificial neural networks.

My Research

I find staggering the Human brain’s ability to make choices that are more flexible, efficient and robust than today’s most powered machines, under scarce resource. The goal of my research is to understand the computations that underlie Human and machine adaptive, efficient inference.

Background: I am currently a research scientist in the Chalk lab (Paris Vision Institute). I have previously worked at the Blue Brain Project (EPFL), and the Gardner lab (Stanford). I have also worked as a Lead Data Scientist, developing and deploying scalable machine-learning software.
